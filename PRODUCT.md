## Platform
web

## Stack
Astro static generation, per Website Starter's established default, producing static HTML/CSS for the brief's Cloudflare Pages target. No server runtime, integration backend or deployment in this portion.

## Users
Parents researching daycare and preschool across Growing Kids' ten Northern Indiana centers.

## Product Purpose
Help parents choose a center and schedule a tour, request information or pre-register. Completed journeys must reach Playground.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This local review draft contains the approved-concept homepage, six ordered differentiators, ELM spotlight, programs, parent videos, tuition support, centers and anniversary content. Supporting pages remain on the existing site. Tour and inquiry handoffs use native destinations; pre-registration remains pending after a paid-waitlist/address mismatch was observed. No provider submissions or CRM receipt tests were performed. Exact location/tour slugs must survive. Final NAP and founding year remain unconfirmed. Current-site content/assets may be reused with explicit newer overrides.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the approved Claude concept and November brief. Standard logo, ELM curriculum, no invented reviews or figures, no waitlist/apply/contact-us wording in final public CTAs. Do not start a new aesthetic interview.

## Evidence on Hand
The outputs directory contains original brief copies, client link workbook, current-site observations, standard logo and recovered concept screenshot/DOM reference. See outputs/source-inventory.md.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled controls, readable contrast and mobile actions that do not cover content are required.
